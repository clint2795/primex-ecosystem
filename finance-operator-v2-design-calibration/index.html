PrimeX Operator UI v2

Design calibration board

Three coherent commercial directions. Semantic colours now adapt separately for dark and light mode so we can judge the actual product system rather than one fixed palette.

Selection rule: judge each direction as a system. Success, attention and critical states are intentionally restrained; they should communicate meaning without turning the product into a traffic-light dashboard.

What to judge now

First decide whether the semantic states feel suitably premium in both themes. Only after that should we narrow A/B/C. We are choosing a durable product language, not chasing isolated colours.
